游戏中的数据

本政策适用于原生iPhone、iPad与Mac版《牌宗 · 五宗归一》。游戏无需注册账号，可以离线游玩。游戏不向开发者服务器上传游戏数据，不包含广告、第三方统计SDK或跨应用跟踪。

成长进度、成就、当前牌局、语言和声音偏好保存在设备上，用于恢复进度及设置。系统备份可能依照你的设备设置包含这些文件；游戏本身不提供账号同步或开发者运营的云存档。

导入、导出和删除

只有在你主动操作时，游戏才会复制内容到剪贴板或使用系统文件选择、导入、导出功能。你决定将文件保存或分享给谁。在iPhone／iPad上删除App及其数据会删除本机存档；Mac上仅移除App可能仍保留用户目录中的存档，可联系支持获取清理步骤。另行保存的导出文件和系统备份需要你在相应位置管理。成长存档导出不包含正在进行的牌局断点。

购买与联系支持

下载购买由Apple处理，我们不接收你的完整付款卡号。若你主动联系 telegram2ai@gmail.com，我们会收到你的邮箱地址、邮件内容和你选择提供的附件，用于回复及处理问题。请仅提供解决问题所需的信息。你可以通过该邮箱提出删除支持通信中个人信息的请求；我们会在适用义务允许的范围内处理。

本支持网站与第三方

本支持网站不使用分析脚本或广告。网站托管服务可能为运行和安全处理访问记录。Apple的商店、系统备份及邮件服务按各自的隐私政策运行。游戏不会通过上述本地设置API建立设备指纹。

儿童与政策更新

游戏不通过应用主动收集儿童的个人信息。请勿在支持邮件中发送不必要的儿童信息。如数据处理方式变化，我们会更新本页并注明日期。隐私问题请联系 telegram2ai@gmail.com。

Data in the game

This policy covers the native iPhone, iPad and Mac versions of PaiZong. The game works offline and requires no account. It does not upload gameplay data to developer servers and contains no advertising, third-party analytics SDKs or cross-app tracking.

Growth progress, achievements, the current run, language and audio preferences stay on your device to restore your progress and settings. System backups may include these files according to your device settings. The game itself does not offer account sync or developer-operated cloud saves.

Import, export and deletion

The game writes to the clipboard or uses system file import and export only when you initiate those actions. You control where exported files are kept or shared. On iPhone and iPad, deleting the app and its data removes local saves. On Mac, removing the app may leave saves in your user directory; contact support for cleanup steps. Manage separately saved exports and system backups in their respective locations. Growth exports do not include the active run.

Purchases and support

Apple processes the app purchase; we do not receive your full payment card details. If you email telegram2ai@gmail.com, we receive your address, message and attachments you choose to send, solely to respond and investigate your request. Send only information needed to resolve the issue. You may request deletion of personal information in support correspondence at that address, subject to applicable retention obligations.

This support website and third parties

This website uses no analytics scripts or advertising. Its hosting provider may process access logs for operation and security. Apple's store, system backup and email services follow their own privacy policies. The game does not use local preference or timing APIs for device fingerprinting.

Children and changes

The app does not actively collect children's personal information. Avoid sending unnecessary information about children in support emails. We will update the date and this policy if data handling changes. Contact telegram2ai@gmail.com for privacy questions.
