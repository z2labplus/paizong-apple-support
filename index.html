如何开始一局

  1. 横屏打开游戏，选择“开始论道”，然后选择难度和牌客。
  2. 依照地图进入关卡，选择奇牌并进入战斗。首次游玩可跟随新手引导。
  3. 点选手牌组成合法牌型，用更强的同类牌型压过对手。关注双方分数和过关目标；提示、过牌、摸牌和道具均受当前回合规则限制。
  4. 获胜后进入结算，继续选择奇牌、购买构筑和挑战下一关。游戏也包含无尽、每日、周常及残局模式。

音乐没有声音

检查设备系统音量；iPhone还需检查静音模式，然后打开游戏顶部音符按钮。音乐和音效分别有开关与音量。耳机断开或音频被中断后，可能需要在声音面板点击继续播放。

存档与换机

成长和进行中的牌局保存在本机。成长存档可从游戏内导出、导入；成长导出不包含进行中的牌局断点。请在删除游戏或换机前自行保存导出的文件。游戏不提供账号或自动云同步。

Mac操作

可调整窗口大小。单击选择手牌，双击快捷出牌；Shift／Command单击或右键可微调单张。游戏菜单列出键盘快捷键。文件导入导出使用系统选择窗口；切换到其他应用后音乐会按声音设置暂停或继续。

语言与购买

游戏内支持18种语言及跟随系统。下载采用一次性付费，实际价格和销售地区以App Store页面为准。购买、账单与退款由Apple处理，可访问 Apple购买支持。

iPad and native Mac releases are in preparation. Check the App Store for current platform availability.

Get help

Email telegram2ai@gmail.com with your device model, operating system and game versions, and steps to reproduce the issue. Do not send passwords, payment card numbers or verification codes.

Start a run

Play in landscape. Start a run, choose a difficulty and character, follow the map, then select a special card and enter battle. Form valid hands and beat the opponent's hand while working toward your score target. The tutorial explains turns, passing, drawing and items. After a win, continue building your deck and advancing through encounters. Endless, daily, weekly and puzzle modes are also available.

Audio

Check your device volume and, on iPhone, the silent switch, then open the music-note button in the game. Music and effects have separate controls. After disconnecting headphones or an audio interruption, use Resume in the sound panel if playback remains paused.

Mac controls

Resize the window to suit your display. Click to select, double-click to play, and use Shift/Command-click or right-click to adjust one card. The Game menu lists keyboard shortcuts. Import and export use system file panels. Background audio follows your sound settings.

Progress and purchases

Progress and the current run are saved locally. Export and import transfer growth progress, not an active run. Keep an exported copy before deleting the app or changing devices; there is no account or automatic cloud sync. The app supports 18 languages plus the system preference. The App Store shows the applicable one-time purchase price and availability. Apple handles payments and refunds at Apple purchase support.
